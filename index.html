Genetic Recombination Mechanics

Synaptonemal Complex (SC)

A protein lattice (SYCP1, SYCP2, SYCP3) zipper-locks non-sister chromatids ~100 nm apart, facilitating precise molecular homology alignment.

Spo11 & Homologous Recombination

Spo11 protein induces targeted double-strand breaks (DSBs). Rad51 and Dmc1 recombinases catalyze strand invasion, generating Holliday Junctions.

Independent Assortment (Mendel's 2nd Law)

Random orientation of bivalents along the metaphase I plate generates $2^n$ gametic variations ($2^{23} \approx 8.39 \times 10^6$ combinations in humans).

Instructional Guide: Mitosis, Meiosis & Cell Cycle Checkpoints

Comprehensive biochemical reference manual and self-assessment suite for cellular replication and oncogenesis.

1. Cell Cycle & Mitotic Engine

The eukaryotic cell cycle comprises Interphase ($\text{G}_1, \text{S}, \text{G}_2$) and Mitotic ($\text{M}$) phase. Oscillations of **Cyclin-CDK heterodimers** drive transitions:

  • G1/S: Cyclin E + CDK2
  • S Phase: Cyclin A + CDK2
  • G2/M: Cyclin B + CDK1 (MPF)

2. Meiotic Recombination & Reassortment

Meiosis generates haploid gametes with unique genetic architecture. Key mechanisms:

  • Synapsis: Homologous pairing in Prophase I via synaptonemal complex.
  • Chiasmata: Reciprocal physical crossing over of non-sister chromatids.
  • Independent Assortment: Random bivalent alignment at Metaphase I plate.

3. Checkpoint Surveillance & Oncogenesis

Checkpoints maintain genomic fidelity:

  • p53 "Guardian of Genome": Upregulates p21 (CDK inhibitor) for repair or Bax/PUMA for apoptosis.
  • Proto-oncogenes vs. Oncogenes: Gain-of-function mutations (e.g., Ras, Cyclin D overexpression) drive unchecked proliferation.
  • Tumor Suppressors: Loss-of-function (e.g., TP53, Rb) bypasses arrest checkpoints.
